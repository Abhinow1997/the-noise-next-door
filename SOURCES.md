One row for every generation that was kept or seriously considered. Rejected outputs are kept as small thumbnails or a contact sheet, not full-size files, because they show the design judgement.

**Who generated what.** The author generated every image and every sound here, and confirmed on 6 Oct 2026 that all the sounds are theirs. Claude Code generated none of them. It only cut the loops and cut-outs described in the Edits column.

**Rejected outputs.** The only rejection recorded so far is ENV-GROUND, which was rejected in the game. No other rejected outputs were kept in the repo.
